Vascudyn XR

(rivaroxaban) extended-release tablets, for oral use

Therapeutic Class Anticoagulant
Dosage Form Tablet, XR
Available Strengths 10mg, 15mg, 20mg
Administration Oral, Once Daily

clinical_notes Indications and Usage

Vascudyn XR is a factor Xa directed anticoagulant indicated for the reduction of risk of stroke and systemic embolism in patients with nonvalvular atrial fibrillation. It is also indicated for the treatment of deep vein thrombosis (DVT), pulmonary embolism (PE), and for the reduction in the risk of recurrence of DVT and of PE.

Important Note: Routine coagulation monitoring is not required. However, in certain rare clinical situations, measuring anti-factor Xa activity may be useful to inform clinical decisions.

medication Dosage and Administration

Indication Recommended Dosage
Nonvalvular Atrial Fibrillation 20 mg once daily with the evening meal.
Treatment of DVT and PE 15 mg orally twice daily with food for the first 21 days, followed by 20 mg orally once daily with food.
Reduction in Risk of Recurrence 10 mg once daily with or without food.
